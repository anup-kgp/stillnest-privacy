Privacy Policy

Stillnest Launcher · Last updated: 29 October 2026

Stillnest Launcher ("the app") is a free home-screen launcher for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no account system, no advertising and no analytics. It does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only in its private app storage on your device:

  • Your settings (clock format, accent colour, wallpaper choice, whether to show app icons).
  • Your favourite apps and the apps you chose to add a mindful pause to.
  • Your Focus session end time.
  • A count of how many apps you opened through the launcher today.
  • If you choose a photo as wallpaper, a resized copy of that photo.
  • A saved copy of your launchable-app list (names only), so the launcher starts quickly.

None of this leaves your device. All of it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Home screen (launcher) role: lets Stillnest handle the Home button. You choose this in Android's own dialog and can change it at any time in Android Settings.
  • Set wallpaper: used only when you tap "Use wallpaper on lock screen" or "Restore original look".
  • Request delete packages: used only to show Android's standard uninstall prompt when you choose "Uninstall" for an app, or "Uninstall Stillnest".

The app reads the list of launchable apps on your device to show them in your app list. This list is used on the device only and is never transmitted.

Photos

If you pick a wallpaper photo, Android's system photo picker gives the app access only to the single photo you choose. The app needs no storage permission.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
